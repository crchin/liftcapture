# LiftCapture Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 30, 2026**

LiftCapture is a velocity-based training app for iPhone and Apple Watch,
currently in beta. It is built privacy-first: your training data lives on
your devices.

## Data stored on your device

Workouts you log (exercises, weights, reps, RPE), workout templates,
sensor recordings from your Apple Watch (motion and heart rate), and all
velocity analysis results are stored locally on your iPhone and Apple
Watch. Analysis runs on your devices — not on a server.

## HealthKit

With your permission, LiftCapture reads your heart rate during workouts
and saves workout sessions to Apple Health. Health data is used only for
the app's features, stays on your device, and is never shared with third
parties or used for advertising or data mining, in accordance with
Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

## Beta telemetry (with your consent)

During the beta, if you agree on the in-app consent screen, LiftCapture
automatically shares limited **derived workout metrics** with the
developer to improve the app:

- Exercise names, weights, reps, and set types you log
- Your RPE (effort) entries
- Computed velocity metrics and detected rep counts
- Your rep-count feedback (correct/incorrect taps)
- App version and workout timestamps

This data is linked only to a **random anonymous identifier** generated
on your device — no name, email, account, or device identifier. It is
transmitted and stored via Apple iCloud (CloudKit) and is used solely to
improve rep detection, velocity estimation, and app quality.

**What is never shared:** raw motion/sensor recordings, heart-rate data,
your location, and your identity. Raw sensor data never leaves your
device unless a future update adds a separate, explicit opt-in — sharing
it will never be automatic.

If you decline consent, the app is fully functional and nothing is
shared.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-in
- No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
- No sale or sharing of data with third parties, ever

## TestFlight

Beta distribution uses Apple TestFlight. Apple's handling of TestFlight
tester information (such as your email invitation) is governed by
Apple's own privacy policy.

## Data deletion

Telemetry is anonymous, so it cannot be traced to you by name. To
request deletion of data associated with your anonymous identifier, or
for any privacy question, contact the developer at the address below.
Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

## Children

LiftCapture is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated during the beta; the effective date above
will change accordingly.
